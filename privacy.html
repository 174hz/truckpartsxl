Commercial Data & Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 22, 2026

1. Scope of Commercial Data Collection

TruckPartsXL processes specialized vehicle logistics metadata to facilitate asset location services within the heavy transportation sector. When interacting with our capture forms, we collect:

  • Fleet Procurement Parameters: Component Descriptions, OEM reference designations, casting stamps, target acquisition budgets, and general breakdown locations.
  • Vehicle Asset Identifiers: 17-digit Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) explicitly provided to match safety-critical fitment requirements.
  • Corporate Contact Records: Corporate names, professional phone coordinates, distribution facility addresses, and commercial email routes.

2. Data Utilization Models

Unlike standard consumer data environments, information submitted to TruckPartsXL is intentionally processed to be broadcast to commercial third parties. Your data is applied as follows:

  • Procurement demands are summarized and converted into dynamic public rows accessible by any commercial yard operator or heavy-duty recycler globally.
  • Sellers submitting match availability authorizations explicitly direct us to communicate their inventory parameters, pricing, and point-of-contact details to the original corporate requestor.

3. Third-Party Data Disclosures & Processing Bridges

TruckPartsXL maintains a streamlined, serverless technology stack utilizing reliable, secure administrative systems. Your tracking information transits through the following entities:

  • Google Workspace Pipelines: Forms and Sheets process and archive lead logs.
  • Stripe Checkout Infrastructure: Premium transaction values ($9.99 boosts) are handled directly by Stripe. TruckPartsXL never stores, handles, or records corporate credit card numbers, banking routing keys, or underlying billing accounts on our own servers.

4. Data Retention and Fleet Erasure Mandates

Commercial active request lines expire or are archived manually as procurement balances settle out. If your fleet group has filled an open sourcing contract or requires the absolute deletion of identifiable records (such as specific cell phone references or VIN patterns) from our historical spreadsheet outputs, issue a direct erasure command to our clearing desk at tpartsxl@gmail.com for removal within 48 business hours.
