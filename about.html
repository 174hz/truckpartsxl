Background

Who We Are

TruckPartsXL is a North American B2B commercial procurement platform purpose-built for the heavy-duty trucking and fleet maintenance industry. We connect logistics operators, fleet managers, and independent repair facilities directly with verified salvage yards, heavy-duty wreckers, and Tier-1 parts distributors across Canada and the United States.

We're not a parts dealer. We're not a broker. We don't touch inventory and we don't take a cut of any sale. What we do is remove the friction between the people who need hard-to-find commercial truck components and the yards that have them sitting on the shelf.

The heavy-duty parts market in North America is massive — and largely still runs on cold calls, fax machines, and word of mouth. TruckPartsXL is changing that. Our live procurement board gives salvage yards real-time visibility into high-intent commercial buyers, and gives buyers a fast, transparent way to broadcast their needs to an entire network at once.

The Platform

How TruckPartsXL Works

The model is straightforward. Buyers post their component requirements to our live board — part name, vehicle specs, VIN, OEM casting number, condition requirements, budget, and region. That listing is instantly broadcast to our verified commercial salvage network.

Salvage yards and parts distributors can browse the full open contracts board for free. When a yard has a match, they pay a small one-time contact unlock fee through Stripe to receive the buyer's direct phone and email. From there, the deal is done between the two parties — no platform involvement, no commission, no middleman.

📦 For Buyers

Post your heavy-duty part requirement in minutes. Your listing is broadcast across our verified salvage and distribution network. Sellers come to you — you never have to cold-call a yard again.

🔧 For Salvage Yards

Monitor the live board for real-time high-intent procurement requests. When you have a match, unlock the buyer's contact with a single $9.99 Stripe payment. No monthly fees, no subscriptions.

The Market

The Industry We Serve

Heavy-Duty Trucking: The Backbone of North America

There are over 3.5 million semi-trucks operating in the United States alone, with hundreds of thousands more operating across Canada. These vehicles collectively haul more than 70% of all freight tonnage transported in North America every year — everything from food and fuel to construction materials and medical supplies.

When a commercial truck goes down, every hour off the road costs money — in missed loads, idle driver time, and contractual penalties. Fleet operators and owner-operators can't afford to wait days for a part to ship from a distant warehouse. They need fast access to local inventory.

That's where the North American heavy-duty salvage ecosystem comes in. Thousands of salvage yards and heavy-duty wreckers across the continent hold millions of dollars in quality used components — engines, transmissions, differentials, turbochargers, cab assemblies, and everything in between. The challenge has always been connecting the right buyer to the right yard, fast. TruckPartsXL solves that problem.

  • ★ Class 6–8 Vehicles: We specialize in heavy-duty commercial vehicles — Class 6 medium-duty trucks through Class 8 semis, including Freightliner, Peterbilt, Kenworth, Mack, Volvo, International, and Western Star.
  • ★ Engine & Drivetrain Focus: The highest-demand categories on our board include diesel engines (Cummins, Detroit, PACCAR MX, Volvo D13), transmissions (Eaton Fuller, Allison), rear differentials, and emission system components.
  • ★ Canada & U.S. Coverage: Our board serves both the Canadian regional market (with a dedicated Ontario/Canada feed) and the broader North American exchange, including cross-border sourcing.
  • ★ OEM & Aftermarket: Buyers can specify OEM casting numbers and part numbers to ensure accurate fitment matching, reducing the risk of compatibility errors on safety-critical components.

Trust & Privacy

Our Commitment to You

We built TruckPartsXL on a simple principle: buyer contact information is private until a seller earns it. Your phone number and email are never published on the public board. They're held securely in our private records and only released to a verified seller who has completed a Stripe payment for that specific listing.

🛡️ Buyer Privacy

Your contact details are never visible on the public board. Only the procurement details — part name, specs, region, and budget — are published. Your identity stays protected until you're ready to connect.

💳 Secure Payments

All payments are processed by Stripe. TruckPartsXL never sees, stores, or handles your credit card information. Every contact unlock transaction is encrypted and fully PCI-DSS compliant.

📋 No Junk Listings

We manually review and archive listings as contracts are fulfilled. Stale or completed requests are removed to keep the board clean and relevant for active salvage yard operators.

⚖️ Legal Compliance

Our platform is governed by Ontario, Canada law and designed to comply with PIPEDA (Canada) and CCPA (California). Full privacy and terms documentation is available on our legal pages.
